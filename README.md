# Warn-The-Sheep
**Question**: Wolves have been reintroduced to Great Britain. You are a sheep farmer, and are now plagued by wolves which pretend to be sheep. Fortunately, you are good at spotting them.  Warn the sheep in front of the wolf that it is about to be eaten. Remember that you are standing at the front of the queue which is at the end of the array, If the wolf is the closest animal to you, return "Pls go away and stop eating my sheep". Otherwise, return "Oi! Sheep number N! You are about to be eaten by a wolf!" where N is the sheep's position in the queue.

**Clarification**: You are at the end of a queue of animals and must watch out for a wolf. Count how many sheep are between the wolf and you to know which sheep is in danger. If the wolf is closest, tell it to go away; otherwise, warn the sheep by giving its position in the line.

**Technique**: I first created a function. Inside the function, I used queue.indexOf("wolf") to determine the position of the wolf in the array. Then, I calculated queue.length - wolfIndex - 1, where queue.length gives the total number of sheeps in the array, wolfIndex is the wolf’s position, and -1 removes the wolf itself from the count,so that the sheep are counted from the wolf toward the end of the queue(in descending order). Finally, I used sheepInFront === 0 to check if there are no sheep in front of the wolf; in that case, the function sends a warning to the wolf.
